# Voice DNA (example)

Optional user-layer file. Copy to `voice-dna.md` in the repo root and customize.

Use this to steer cover letters, outreach, and follow-ups toward your natural voice while keeping facts grounded in `cv.md` and `article-digest.md`.

## Rules

- Short paragraphs. Get to the point.
- Use contractions and direct "I" / "you" language.
- Be specific. Numbers and names beat vague claims.
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- Never invent metrics or projects not in your source files.

## Banned filler

- "passionate about", "synergy", "leverage", "utilize", "spearheaded" (unless it is literally in your CV)
- "I am excited to apply" openings
- Generic "team player" / "fast learner" without proof

## When to apply

- Cover letters and recruiter outreach: full voice rules
- CV bullets and ATS summary: anti-slop only, keep formal keyword density
